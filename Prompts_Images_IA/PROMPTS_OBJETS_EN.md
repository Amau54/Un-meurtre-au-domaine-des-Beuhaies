# Per-prop prompts (EN)

> One block per prop. **Copy the "Prompt"** into Gemini / ChatGPT / Midjourney.
> For long text, use **method (B)** from the guide: generate the template, then overlay the **French verbatim** provided.
> ⚠️ **All text on the props stays in French.** The English here is only the styling instruction.
> Style reminder everywhere: *period flat-lay photo, June 1925, yellowed ivory laid paper, brown-black ink, soft raking light, film grain, French text, no errors.*

## PROP 01 — Note by the bench (blackmail of Lord Louis-Marc)
**Where:** under a stone near the garden bench, **slightly charred**.
**Format:** `3:4` · **Tool:** method (B) recommended (long text).

**Prompt:**
> Top-down macro photograph of a **handwritten 1925 letter** lying on a stone bench in a garden at late afternoon. Small sheet of yellowed ivory laid paper, English roundhand in faded brown-black ink. The **lower-right edge is charred and scorched**, as if just saved from fire, with blackened margins and a missing corner. Strong fold lines, dog-eared, a few stains. A **grey pebble** rests partly on the corner. Soft raking light, long shadows, analog film grain, sepia palette. No modern elements. `--style raw --ar 3:4`

**French verbatim (to overlay):**
> Mon cher Lord Louis-Marc,
> On porte certains secrets cinq années entières avant qu'une plume étrangère ne les surprenne. Je sais — pour Gautier, et pour vous.
> Cinq cents livres, samedi soir sans faute, et la rumeur s'éteint dans l'œuf. Marie ne saura jamais — si vous savez payer.
> — L.
