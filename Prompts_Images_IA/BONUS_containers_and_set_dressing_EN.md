# Bonus — containers & set dressing (EN)

> Prompts for the **containers** that hide the clues, and for **mood shots** of the investigation table. Same art direction (June 1925, flat-lay or still life, film grain, sepia palette). No rendered text needed here.

---

## B1 — The padlocked rattan trunk
**Role:** holds the birth certificate (03), the 5 June letter (04) and the sealed note (04b).

**Prompt:**
> Still life of a **small 1920s woven rattan trunk**, patinated, shut with an old **brass padlock**, sitting in garden grass under late-afternoon light. Worn leather straps, reinforced corners. Shallow depth of field, film grain, honey-and-sepia palette. `--style raw --ar 3:2`

---

## B2 — The digitalin vial
**Role:** carries label (05), in Baptiste's coat pocket.

**Prompt:**
> Macro of a **small 1925 amber glass pharmaceutical vial**, cork stopper, **cream apothecary label with a thin black border**, resting on dark wool (a coat lining). Soft reflections on the glass, shallow depth of field, film grain, amber-sepia palette. `--style raw --ar 1:1`

---

## B3 — The coat on the bar stool
**Role:** hides blackmail items 05 (vial) and 06 (Vienna).

**Prompt:**
> Still life of an **elegant 1920s man's coat** draped over a wooden **bar stool** in a hushed salon. A corner of folded paper and the neck of a vial peek from a pocket. Warm dim light, shallow depth of field, film grain, brown-sepia palette. `--style raw --ar 3:2`

---

## B4 — Lord Gautier's briefcase
**Role:** hides the rendezvous note (07), in the hallway closet.

**Prompt:**
> Still life of a **1920s leather briefcase** half-open at the bottom of a dark closet, patinated leather, brass clasps, a handwritten note visible inside. Contrasty light from the ajar door, film grain, leather-and-shadow palette. `--style raw --ar 3:2`

---

## B5 — Léa's workshop annex (Act IV)
**Role:** central Act IV setting (notebook 08-09, ledger 09b, notes 10, bundles 11-12).

**Prompt:**
> Wide still life of a **1925 writer's desk** in a workshop annex: an open leather notebook, an **inkwell and dip pen**, scattered handwritten sheets, a **bundle of letters tied with pink silk ribbon**, a box lined with Japanese paper, a green-shaded lamp. Dust in a shaft of late-afternoon light, film grain, sepia-and-bottle-green palette. `--style raw --ar 3:2`

---

## B6 — Mood shot: the clue table (cover / teaser)
**Role:** key visual to present the game to guests.

**Prompt:**
> Neat top-down flat-lay of a **set of 1925 investigation clues** arranged on **bottle-green felt**: yellowed handwritten letters, a fold sealed with **burgundy wax**, an old birth certificate, an **amber apothecary vial**, an open leather notebook, a brass padlock, an **antique magnifying glass**, a few fresh raspberries in one corner. Soft raking light, elegant shadows, film grain, sepia-ivory-burgundy palette, chic murder-party atmosphere. `--style raw --ar 3:2`

---

## B7 — The basket of raspberries (nod to Amaury's garden)
**Role:** ambiance / table dressing.

**Prompt:**
> Still life of a **small wicker basket of fresh raspberries** on écru linen, beside a handwritten card. Soft natural garden light, dewdrops, shallow depth of field, film grain, green-and-muted-red palette. `--style raw --ar 1:1`
